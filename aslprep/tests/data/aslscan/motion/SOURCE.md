# motion

Written for ASLPrep (integration tier). F7. The F1 protocol at multiband 3 (13 slice groups 80 ms apart) with a known rigid head-motion trajectory (a drift to 1.5 mm and 1.5 degrees, plus 2 mm / 2 degree spikes on volumes 6, 11 and 17), delta-M SNR 5 per pair, the T1w header offset by a known rigid transform (3, -4, 2 mm; 5, -3, 4 degrees), and no anatomical derivatives, so ASLPrep preprocesses the anatomy itself.
