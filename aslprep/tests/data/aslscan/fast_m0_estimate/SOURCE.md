# fast_m0_estimate

Written for ASLPrep (fast tier). PCASL calibrated with a single M0Estimate value.

BIDS defines `M0Estimate` as the M0 of arterial blood. The value is 6000 / 0.9: a tissue M0 of
6000, near the simulated grey and white matter M0 (about 6500 and 5800), divided by the
blood-brain partition coefficient.
